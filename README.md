## What is Noor?

Noor is an AI-powered career and skills advisor built specifically for secondary school students aged 14–18. It lives inside the school ecosystem and guides every student through a personalised journey — helping them choose the right subjects, discover their strengths, and build the skills that actually matter for their future.

Where most schools rely on a single counsellor repeating the same conversations across hundreds of students, Noor scales that guidance to every student — intelligently, privately, and without extra admin.

---

## The Problem Noor Solves

- Students pick subjects without understanding where they lead
- Counsellors are stretched — one person supporting hundreds of students, with no time for meaningful guidance
- Career advice lives in long PDFs, not interactive tools built for Gen Z
- Parents have no visibility into their child's academic direction
- Schools have no cohort-level data on student confidence or career intent

---

## How It Works

### 💬 AI-Guided Conversation
Noor asks 12–15 carefully designed questions covering academic preferences, personal strengths, extracurricular interests, and career aspirations. The conversation adapts to each response — no forms, no boxes to tick. About 5 minutes, and it feels completely natural.

### 🌐 Career Domains That Feel Real
Instead of overwhelming students with 800 job titles, Noor maps futures into 12 engaging career domains designed for Gen Z — like *Tech Wizards*, *Creators & Storytellers*, and *Mind & Health Heroes*. Each domain includes real career examples, salary ranges, and the skills employers actually look for.

### ⭐ Gamified Skills Journeys
Short 10-minute missions, badges, and progress bars — each tied to the 11 employability skills students will actually need. Built on evidence-based learning science: spaced repetition, active recall, and immediate feedback. Students earn real credentials they can add to their portfolio.

### 📊 Counsellor Dashboard
Real-time cohort analytics: confidence levels per year group, popular career domains, students who haven't completed their assessment, and flags for students who need additional support — all visible in under 60 seconds.
